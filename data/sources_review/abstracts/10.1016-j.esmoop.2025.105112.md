# LBA1 Adjuvant pertuzumab or placebo + trastuzumab + chemotherapy (P or Pla + T + CT) in patients (pts) with early HER2-positive operable breast cancer in APHINITY: Final analysis at 11.3 years’ median follow-up

**Autores:** Loibl S.; Piccart M.; Clark E.; Viale G.; Caballero C.; Henry C.; Tomasello G.; Fein L.E.; Gnant M.I.; Kuemmel S.; Plummer C.; Im S-A.; Yang Y-S.; Takano T.; De la Haba Rodriguez J.; McConnell R.; de Azambuja E.; Procter M.; Bines J.; Gelber R.D.
**Fonte:** ESMO Open; 2025;10:105112 (Abstract LBA1, ESMO Breast Cancer 2025 – open access)
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esmoop.2025.105112

## Resumo (Abstract) — extraído da página do publisher (LBA1 ESMO Breast 2025)

### Background
The primary analysis of the randomised, phase III, double-blind APHINITY trial (NCT01358877) showed that adjuvant P + T + CT [pertuzumab + trastuzumab + quimioterapia] significantly improved invasive disease-free survival (IDFS) vs placebo (Pla) + T + CT, leading to P + T + CT becoming the new standard of care for pts at high risk of relapse. However, no significant overall survival (OS) benefit was demonstrated in prior interim analyses.

### Methods
4804 pts were enrolled in the intention-to-treat population from Nov 2011–Aug 2013; 2400 were assigned to P + T + CT; 2404 to Pla + T + CT. Final, prespecified analysis of OS, with updated descriptive IDFS and cardiac safety data.

### Results
With 11.3 years’ median follow-up (clinical cutoff 28 Nov 2024), there were 452 deaths overall; 205 (8.5%) in the P arm vs 247 (10.3%) in the Pla arm. OS: HR 0.83 (95% CI 0.69–1.00; p = 0.044; boundary for significance = 0.0496); 10-year OS 91.6% vs 89.8%. Significant OS improvement was seen in node-positive (HR 0.79; 95% CI 0.64–0.97) but not node-negative (HR 0.99; 95% CI 0.66–1.49) disease, and in HR-positive (HR 0.76; 95% CI 0.60–0.97) but not HR-negative (HR 0.94; 95% CI 0.70–1.26) disease. Updated IDFS at 11.3 years: HR 0.79 (95% CI 0.68–0.92); 10-year IDFS 87.2% vs 83.8%; benefit clinically meaningful in N+ subgroup, none in N–. Rate of primary cardiac events < 1%; no new cardiac safety concerns.

### Conclusions
In this prespecified OS analysis with longer follow-up (median, 11.3 years), a statistically significant OS improvement was observed by adding adjuvant P to T + CT. The IDFS benefit was maintained (clinically meaningful in N+; none in N–). No new cardiac safety concerns emerged. Supports the benefit of P in the early breast cancer setting.

**Clinical trial identification:** NCT01358877.
