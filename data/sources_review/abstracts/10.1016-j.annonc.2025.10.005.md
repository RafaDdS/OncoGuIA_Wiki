# Overall survival with abemaciclib in early breast cancer (monarchE — resultados definitivos de OS)

**Autores:** Johnston SRD, et al.
**Fonte:** Annals of Oncology; 2026;37(2):155-165
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2025.10.005
**PMID:** 41110697
**NCT:** NCT03155997

## Resumo (Abstract) — via PubMed

**Background:** Adjuvant abemaciclib combined with endocrine therapy (ET) significantly improved invasive disease-free survival (IDFS) in patients with hormone receptor (HR)-positive, human epidermal growth factor 2 (HER2)-negative, node-positive, high-risk early breast cancer (EBC). The impact on overall survival (OS) remained unknown.

**M&eacute;todos:** In the phase III monarchE trial (NCT03155997), patients received ET for at least 5 years with or without abemaciclib for 2 years. In this article, the primary OS results are reported (a key secondary endpoint), plus updated estimates of IDFS and distant relapse-free survival (DRFS). Overall, 5637 patients underwent randomization, with 2808 assigned to abemaciclib-ET, and 2829 to ET.

**Resultados:** In the intent-to-treat population, with a median follow-up of 76.2 months, abemaciclib-ET resulted in a 15.8% lower risk of death than ET [661 deaths; HR 0.842, 95% CI 0.722&#8211;0.981, P = 0.027], meeting the prespecified boundary for significance. The 7-year OS was 86.8% with abemaciclib-ET and 85.0% with ET (absolute difference, 1.8%). OS benefit was consistent across prespecified subgroups. In addition to patients who had already died of metastatic disease, fewer patients in the abemaciclib-ET arm were living with metastatic disease compared with the ET arm (6.4% vs 9.4%). Sustained improvement was demonstrated in IDFS and DRFS (HR 0.734, 95% CI 0.657&#8211;0.820 and HR 0.746, 95% CI 0.662&#8211;0.840, respectively). Seven-year IDFS was 77.4% with abemaciclib-ET and 70.9% with ET (absolute difference, 6.5%) and 7-year DRFS were 80.0% and 74.9% (absolute difference, 5.1%). The long-term safety data compiled did not support any concerns of delayed toxicities.

**Conclus&otilde;es:** Adjuvant abemaciclib-ET resulted in a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in OS compared with ET in patients with HR-positive, HER2-negative, node-positive, high-risk EBC. At 7 years, abemaciclib-ET continued to demonstrate a sustained IDFS and DRFS benefit.
